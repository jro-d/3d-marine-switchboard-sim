Help & Walkthrough

MCA COSWP Ch. 22 & STCW A-III/6. Work on equipment above 1,000 V follows the five golden rules, in this order.
1. Disconnect completely

Trip the VCB and rack the truck to DISCONNECTED 0/20. Isolate every source, including 440 V secondary back-feed.

2. Secure against re-connection

Padlock the racking port, extract the Castell key, transfer it to the earth switch, lock keys in the safety key box and post danger tags.

3. Verify dead — prove, test, prove

Select the 6.6 kV Wand ([7]), prove it on the Test Bench Proving Unit, walk to the open bay and prove L1, L2, L3 directly on the terminals or station panel, then prove again.

4. Earth and short-circuit

Close the integral earth switch and/or apply CME leads — PE first, then L1, L2, L3.

5. Guard adjacent live parts & issue the permit

Padlock the red busbar shutter and issue a signed Permit-to-Work or Sanction-for-Test at the PTW Desk.

01 · Bow Thruster motor isolation & PTW

PPE → trip → rack out → extract K1-BT & padlock → close/padlock earth switch → lock key box → open door, padlock red shutter, prove–test–prove (L1, L2, L3) → issue PTW.

02 · Transformer dual-end isolation

Isolate the 440 V ACB-TR1-LV first (back-feed trap), then TR1-HV. Insert both K-HV and K-LV to close the earth switch, apply CME, issue PTW.

03 · Sanction-for-Test & 5 kV PI test

Cancel PTW → issue SFT → open earth switch → run test (PI 2.49) → discharge 4,850 V DC to 0 V → re-earth → cancel SFT.

04 · De-isolation & restoration

Cancel PTW → remove CME phases first, PE last → close door → open earth switch, return K1-BT → function test at TEST 10/20 → rack to SERVICE 20/20.

How do I move and interact in the switchroom?
Walk with W A S D or arrow keys (Shift to sprint), look by dragging the mouse (or moving mouse in 1st-person), press V to switch 1st/3rd-person, press E near stations or click L1/L2/L3/PE terminals directly when the cubicle door is open.
How do I use the 6.6 kV proving wand on L1–L3?
Equip the 6.6 kV Wand ([7]), prove it at the Test Bench, walk to the open cubicle (BT-M1 / TR1-HV), and either click the copper busbar terminals directly in 3D or trigger Prove L1, Prove L2, Prove L3 in the bay action panel, then prove once more at the Test Bench.
Why is the racking crank or earth switch blocked?
The racking port is interlocked while the VCB is closed or padlocked, and Castell keys only release at DISCONNECTED 0/20.
What is the reverse CME rule?
Remove phase clamps (L1–L3) first and the PE clamp last, so the lead is never floating at induced voltage in your hands.
